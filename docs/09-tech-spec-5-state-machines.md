# Technical Specification #5 — Detailed State Machines (LOCKED)

## 1. Golden Rule
Status AI ya normal CRUD update se directly change nahin hoga. Wrong: UPDATE opportunities SET stage='WON'. Correct: Command→Transition Request→Current State→Transition Allowed?→Guards→Permission→Policy if required→Transaction→State Change→History→Domain Event. State changes are business actions, not field edits.

## 2. Common Transition Architecture
Every important transition: FROM/TO/COMMAND/ACTOR/GUARDS/REQUIRED DATA/SIDE EFFECTS/EVENT/REASON. Example Campaign READY→ACTIVE: command StartCampaign, guards audience valid/mailbox healthy/policy allows/not globally paused, event CampaignStarted.

## 3. Transition Result
ALLOWED/BLOCKED/WAITING/REQUIRES_APPROVAL/INVALID_TRANSITION. Frontend aur AI dono ko exact result milega.

## 4-10. Company Lifecycle
DISCOVERED→RESEARCHING→ACTIVE→CUSTOMER. Alternatives: any valid state→DISQUALIFIED with reason_code (OUTSIDE_ICP/CLOSED_BUSINESS/WRONG_MARKET/DUPLICATE/NOT_TARGETABLE/MANUAL); CUSTOMER→FORMER_CUSTOMER; ANY VALID STATE→ARCHIVED. DISCOVERED: canonical company created, intelligence incomplete; next RESEARCHING/ACTIVE/DISQUALIFIED/ARCHIVED. RESEARCHING: underway, not sales-qualified; failure doesn't auto-disqualify (research_status=BLOCKED possible). ACTIVE: usable canonical account, not interested/qualified/opportunity/customer. CUSTOMER: requires evidence (OpportunityWon or authorized manual import), not AI guess. ARCHIVED: hidden from active workflows, no new autonomous prospecting, history retained; NOT same as suppression; unarchive possible.

## 11-20. Discovery Mission State Machine
DRAFT→READY→PLANNING→DISCOVERING→RESOLVING→ENRICHING→ASSESSING_COVERAGE→(more search→DISCOVERING | enough/saturated→COMPLETED). Global alternates: PAUSED/WAITING/BLOCKED/FAILED/CANCELLED. DRAFT→READY guards: market exists/geography valid/industry+objective/mode selected/exhaust budget limits. READY→PLANNING trigger StartDiscoveryMission, check user/AI authority, provider availability, workspace status, budget. PLANNING→DISCOVERING: planner must create at least one valid query/source strategy; no usable provider→BLOCKED not FAILED. DISCOVERING→RESOLVING: after round; raw observations to entity resolution; UI distinguishes raw observations vs unique resolved companies. RESOLVING→ENRICHING optional based on objective/priority/gaps/budget; can go directly to ASSESSING_COVERAGE. ASSESSING_COVERAGE evaluates new unique yield/unsearched families/provider coverage/duplicate ratio/budget/rounds/human stop → CONTINUE/COMPLETE/WAIT/BLOCK. COMPLETED: defined process finished per stopping criteria, NOT "every business guaranteed found"; store coverage_confidence/completion_reason/queries_exhausted/sources_used. Mission PAUSED intentional vs WAITING expected temporary dependency (rate limit reset in 2h). BLOCKED cannot continue until dependency/action changes; FAILED unrecoverable processing failure.

## 21-27. Campaign State Machine
DRAFT→READY→ACTIVE→COMPLETED; operational: ACTIVE↔PAUSED, READY→BLOCKED, ACTIVE→BLOCKED, DRAFT/READY/PAUSED→ARCHIVED, COMPLETED→ARCHIVED. DRAFT freely editable, cannot send. READY: preflight passed (objective/audience/offer/sequence/mailbox/suppression/policy configured), not yet sending. ACTIVE: StartCampaign after permission/autonomy/approval/global outbound/provider health/budget. PAUSED: no new outbound/FU execution; inbound continues; research may continue; resume requires revalidation. BLOCKED: mailbox disconnected/policy violation/config invalidated/budget unavailable; AI surfaces why + what resolves. COMPLETED: audience exhausted/end condition/explicit completion/goal reached; completed campaign cannot become ACTIVE; to rerun → Clone/New Version/New Campaign.

## 28-33. Enrollment State Machine
ELIGIBLE→ENROLLED→ACTIVE; exits REPLIED/COMPLETED/PAUSED/REMOVED/SUPPRESSED/BLOCKED. ELIGIBLE→ENROLLED guards: company active, person/contact appropriate, not suppressed, no account conflict, criteria match, frequency cap ok. ENROLLED→ACTIVE: automation allowed to begin, but every step fresh eligibility check. ACTIVE→REPLIED: genuine prospect reply → cancel pending cold follow-ups → campaign loses autonomous cold-sequence ownership. SUPPRESSED: hard state for that campaign/contact; unsubscribe/DNC/legal/complaint policy; no sequence resume. BLOCKED: invalid email/stale contact/company conflict/policy block; can become eligible later.

## 34-45. Conversation State Machine
Core: COLD→CONTACTED→REPLIED→ENGAGED→QUALIFYING→QUALIFIED→MEETING_REQUESTED→MEETING_BOOKED. Alternates: NURTURE/CLOSED/SUPPRESSED/ESCALATED. Separate from mode AUTO/ASSIST/HUMAN — status=QUALIFYING+mode=HUMAN valid. CONTACTED→REPLIED requires actual genuine inbound message, not open/click/AI guess. REPLIED→ENGAGED requires meaningful two-way commercial interaction; a "remove me" becomes SUPPRESSED, not ENGAGED. ENGAGED→QUALIFYING when enough context to collect/confirm commercial requirements; not mandatory. QUALIFYING→QUALIFIED when configured requirements met (need known, timeline usable, appropriate stakeholder/contact, project commercially relevant); budget may be optional; no forced BANT. MEETING_REQUESTED requires actual meeting intent ("Can we talk tomorrow?"/"Send calendar"/"Let's schedule"), not "they seem interested". MEETING_BOOKED requires Meeting entity exists + provider confirmation if provider-backed; not AI statement. NURTURE: not now/future timing/long-term interest/temporary delay; store next meaningful date/window. CLOSED requires reason_code NOT_INTERESTED/BAD_FIT/RESOLVED/DEAL_LOST/MANUAL/OTHER; not necessarily suppressed. SUPPRESSED: communication restriction, hard suppression support, AI cannot reopen. ESCALATED: sensitive complaint/contract/pricing authority/legal/security/high-value unusual request; may coexist with mode=HUMAN.

## 46-47. Conversation Mode State Machine
AUTO↔ASSIST↔HUMAN controlled. AUTO→HUMAN can happen automatically due risk; side effects: cancel/block pending AI replies, preserve drafts, create attention item if needed, audit takeover. HUMAN→AUTO requires authorized user/policy + revalidation.

## 48-55. Opportunity + Stage Rules
Opportunity separate: status OPEN/WON/LOST/ARCHIVED + pipeline_stage for commercial position. Default pipeline: NEW→CONTACTED→ENGAGED→QUALIFIED→MEETING→PROPOSAL→NEGOTIATION→WON; LOST/NURTURE alternate. Each stage defines required_fields/required_evidence/allowed_previous_stages/approval_requirement. Example →PROPOSAL requires commercial need/primary contact/offer-service. Skipping stages possible where valid (inbound referral immediately requests proposal → NEW→QUALIFIED if evidence exists); history records actual path, no fake historical stages. WON: MarkOpportunityWon; guards open + valid revenue fields + authority + commercial confirmation; transaction sets status=WON+stage=Won semantic+StageHistory+RevenueEvent if applicable+outbox event. WON terminal: WON→NEGOTIATION not normal; mistake → explicit correct/reopen workflow with audit/reason. LOST requires loss_reason; optional competitor/future_revisit_date/notes. LOST→Reopen requires reason + permission, creates history, never silently set status=OPEN.

## 56-58. Proposal State Machine
DRAFT→IN_REVIEW→APPROVED→SENT→(VIEWED/ACCEPTED/DECLINED/EXPIRED/WITHDRAWN where provider supports). Once SENT, don't mutate; changes create Proposal v2; v1 historical. APPROVED→SENT revalidate: approval still valid?/opportunity open?/recipient valid?/suppression?/commercial terms unchanged?.

## 59-64. Meeting State Machine
PROPOSED→PENDING_CONFIRMATION→BOOKED→(COMPLETED/NO_SHOW/CANCELLED); optional BOOKED→RESCHEDULING→BOOKED. PROPOSED: meeting intent exists, no confirmed provider event yet. BOOKED requires provider confirmation or authorized manually-confirmed external meeting; store provider_event_id/start_at/end_at/timezone. Reschedule: request→current availability→new slot→provider update→MeetingRescheduled; old meeting historical. CANCELLED reason/source PROSPECT/TEAM/SYSTEM/PROVIDER/MANUAL; doesn't automatically mean lost opportunity. NO_SHOW can trigger follow-up eligibility/task/opportunity health update, not automatic Opportunity LOST.

## 65-66. Qualification State
NOT_STARTED→IN_PROGRESS→SUFFICIENT; alternatives BLOCKED/STALE. Not permanently complete; facts can age/change. STALE: prospect returns 10 months later; old budget/timeline/decision process marks stale/revalidate.

## 67-76. Signal + Hypothesis + Fact + Contact + Memory + Commitment + Task
Signal: CANDIDATE→DETECTED→VALIDATED→(ACTIONABLE/MONITOR/DISMISSED)→later ACTED_ON/EXPIRED. CANDIDATE raw possible change; VALIDATED credible change but not necessarily commercial intent; ACTIONABLE relevant to current business objective, not "definitely buying"; EXPIRED no longer drives current Why Now. Opportunity Hypothesis: CANDIDATE→ACTIVE→(SUPPORTED/WEAKENED/INVALIDATED/EXPIRED)+CONVERTED. Fact: CANDIDATE→ACTIVE→(CONFLICTED/SUPERSEDED/INVALIDATED/STALE); never delete old fact. ContactPoint: DISCOVERED→UNVERIFIED→VALID/INVALID/RISKY/CATCH_ALL/UNKNOWN/STALE/SUPPRESSED. Verification distinct from outreach permission; valid email can still be suppressed. Memory: CANDIDATE→ACTIVE→(CONFLICTED/STALE/SUPERSEDED/INVALIDATED/ARCHIVED); "Call me in November" can expire after timing window. Commitment: OPEN→WAITING→DUE→COMPLETED; alternatives BROKEN/CANCELLED. Commitment DUE triggers EvaluateCommitment; scheduler doesn't auto-send. Task: OPEN→IN_PROGRESS→COMPLETED; branches WAITING/BLOCKED/CANCELLED. Task BLOCKED requires blocked_reason/blocked_by; dependency resolved → BLOCKED→OPEN/IN_PROGRESS. AttentionItem: OPEN→ACKNOWLEDGED→RESOLVED; alternatives DISMISSED/SUPERSEDED; not automatically a Task.

## 77-82. Approval + External Action
Approval PENDING→APPROVED/REJECTED/EXPIRED/INVALIDATED/CANCELLED; terminal for that approval version. APPROVED ≠ EXECUTED: APPROVED→Continuation→Revalidation→External Action; UI distinguishes Approved/Executed/Blocked after approval. INVALIDATED when material fingerprint changes (approved $5000, current $7500 → invalidation + new approval). External Action: PREPARED→WAITING_APPROVAL→APPROVED→QUEUED→EXECUTING→SUCCEEDED; alternates WAITING/BLOCKED/FAILED/CANCELLED. Can go PREPARED→APPROVED when policy says ACT. Before EXECUTING: acquire idempotency protection/revalidate current state/check kill switch/check provider health, only then side effect. FAILED metadata: failure_type/retryable/attempt_count/last_error; retryable FAILED creates new execution attempt under same logical action identity. SUCCEEDED terminal; same idempotency_key never produces another side effect.

## 83-87. Agent Task + AI Plan + Goal
AgentTask: PENDING→QUEUED→RUNNING→(COMPLETED/PARTIAL/WAITING_TOOL/WAITING_HUMAN/BLOCKED/FAILED/CANCELLED). WAITING_TOOL example: enrichment provider rate-limited; no human. WAITING_HUMAN example: need approved pricing answer → may create AttentionItem/KnowledgeGap/ApprovalRequest. PARTIAL useful: website found✓ owner✓ verified email✗ → retain useful result + decide next mission. AI Plan: DRAFT→ACTIVE→(PAUSED/COMPLETED/SUPERSEDED/CANCELLED/BLOCKED). Plan v1 ACTIVE → new v2 → v1 SUPERSEDED → v2 ACTIVE; historical decisions remain explainable. Goal: DRAFT→ACTIVE→(ACHIEVED/MISSED/CANCELLED); recommended separate status from health: status DRAFT/ACTIVE/ACHIEVED/MISSED/CANCELLED, health ON_TRACK/AT_RISK/BLOCKED.

## 88-90. Knowledge State
DRAFT→IN_REVIEW→APPROVED→(STALE/CONFLICT/ARCHIVED); from review REJECTED. Only valid/effective approved revisions should become autonomous answer authority; draft KB content should not silently influence prospect replies. CONFLICT example: pricing doc A setup $500 vs B $750 → relevant autonomous pricing answer BLOCK/HUMAN until resolved. STALE ≠ false; freshness requirement expired or source needs review; high-risk use can be blocked. KnowledgeGap: OPEN→ANSWERED→INCORPORATED; alternatives DISMISSED/DUPLICATE. ANSWERED ≠ published KB; human answer may still require approval.

## 91-94. Integration + Provider Health + Experiment + Learning
Integration: DISCONNECTED→CONNECTING→ACTIVE; operational: DEGRADED/AUTH_EXPIRED/RATE_LIMITED/ERROR/DISABLED. DEGRADED example read works/send failing → capabilities should have health where practical. AUTH_EXPIRED: stop affected external actions/create reconnect attention/preserve queued work; don't retry OAuth failure forever. Experiment: DRAFT→READY→RUNNING→(PAUSED/COMPLETED/STOPPED/INVALID). READY requires hypothesis/control-treatment/target cohort/primary metric/guardrails/sample rules. RUNNING: assignments locked per design, don't casually move prospects between variants. Guardrail breach: RUNNING→PAUSED or STOPPED depending severity + human attention if required; learning engine cannot override safety. Learning: CANDIDATE→UNDER_REVIEW→VALIDATED→ADOPTED; alternatives REJECTED/STALE/SUPERSEDED. VALIDATED ≠ ADOPTED: validated learning may still require approval before production adoption. Suppression: ACTIVE/REVOKED where legally/policy appropriate; some non-revocable. AI cannot revoke suppression; human/governance controlled.

## 95-96. Provider Health + Workspace Outbound State + Emergency Stop
ProviderHealth: HEALTHY/DEGRADED/UNAVAILABLE/AUTH_REQUIRED/RATE_LIMITED. RATE_LIMITED→WAIT; AUTH_REQUIRED→BLOCK+HUMAN; UNAVAILABLE→RETRY/WAIT. WorkspaceOutbound: ACTIVE/PAUSED/EMERGENCY_STOP — sits above campaigns. Campaign ACTIVE + ExternalAction APPROVED cannot execute when WorkspaceOutbound=PAUSED. EMERGENCY_STOP requires high authority; no new external outbound execution; inbound continues. Emergency resume: request→authority→system health→queued action revalidation→ACTIVE; do not auto-blast queued actions.

## 97-99. Guards + Ordering + Reason + History
Reusable guards: PermissionGuard/WorkspaceGuard/SuppressionGuard/ProviderHealthGuard/BudgetGuard/FreshnessGuard/EvidenceGuard/RelationshipGuard/PolicyGuard/ApprovalGuard/IdempotencyGuard/ConcurrencyGuard. For external actions, cheap/hard checks first: Workspace/Kill Switch→Suppression→Permission/Authority→Entity State→Relationship Conflict→Freshness→Policy→Budget→Provider Health→Idempotency. Important transitions record reason_code/reason_summary/actor/timestamp/correlation_id. State history: StateTransitionHistory (entity_type/entity_id/from/to/command/actor/reason/occurred_at/correlation_id). Opportunity may keep specialized history too. Enables calculating how long opportunities in proposal, campaigns blocked, qualification duration, AI escalation frequency, approval times.

## 100-104. Invalid vs Policy Block + WAIT vs BLOCK + Failure ≠ Business Rejection + Event Coupling + Audit
INVALID_TRANSITION: Campaign COMPLETED→ACTIVE → suggest Clone campaign rather than silently mutate. Policy block ≠ invalid transition: READY→ACTIVE is structurally valid but needs approval → REQUIRES_APPROVAL. WAIT expected to resolve automatically (rate limit, scheduled time, temporary outage); BLOCK explicit change/intervention needed (no mailbox, KB conflict, budget approval, missing authority). API timeout is operational failure, not campaign policy block; don't mix technical failures and business decisions. Every successful meaningful transition emits event: Campaign READY→ACTIVE=CampaignStarted; Conversation CONTACTED→REPLIED=ReplyReceived/ConversationStateChanged; Opportunity OPEN→WON=OpportunityWon; Meeting PENDING→BOOKED=MeetingBooked. Generic state-changed events may coexist but business events remain explicit. Transition also produces Domain event/Activity/Audit with actor/reason.

## 105-108. UI/API + AI + NL + Invalid + Cross-Entity Invariants
Frontend doesn't decide available buttons manually; backend returns allowed_actions based on current state + permissions. AI can ask getAllowedActions(entity) instead of hallucinating "reopen this completed campaign". NL: "GreenScape wali campaign dobara start kar do" with Campaign=COMPLETED → cannot execute invalid transition → AI responds with valid option "Clone campaign using previous configuration". Cross-entity invariants: Conversation=MEETING_BOOKED normally implies Meeting BOOKED exists. Opportunity.status=WON requires Won semantic stage and vice versa. Enrollment=REPLIED means pending cold follow-up actions must not execute even if stale BullMQ job exists; execution-time guard catches. Suppression=ACTIVE wins over Campaign ACTIVE/Enrollment ACTIVE/Approval APPROVED/ExternalAction QUEUED; workspace emergency stop wins over all normal outbound permissions. Conversation.mode=HUMAN blocks autonomous conversational sends unless explicitly separate policy/action says otherwise. Knowledge=CONFLICT for required pricing info blocks autonomous pricing commitment. Calendar=UNAVAILABLE means AI cannot transition meeting to provider-confirmed BOOKED.

## 109-113. State Reconciliation Jobs + Gmail Reconciliation + Implementation Pattern + No One Universal Engine + TS Types + Transition Commands
External provider drift: Calendar event deleted outside app → Provider Sync→detect difference→MeetingReconciliationRequired→update state safely→audit. Gmail missed webhook → periodic sync→new provider message IDs→inbound dedupe→process missing messages→state machine catches up. NestJS pattern: Command Handler→Load Aggregate→StateMachine.canTransition()→Run Guards→Aggregate.transition()→Repository.save()→Outbox.add() inside one transaction. Don't build one universal giant JSON state engine; share utilities but explicit domain designs: CampaignStateMachine/ConversationStateMachine/OpportunityStateMachine/ExternalActionStateMachine. TypeScript strongly typed: type CampaignStatus='DRAFT'|'READY'|'ACTIVE'|'PAUSED'|'BLOCKED'|'COMPLETED'|'ARCHIVED'; Prisma/PostgreSQL enums. Explicit transition commands: PrepareCampaign/StartCampaign/PauseCampaign/ResumeCampaign/CompleteCampaign/TakeOverConversation/ReturnConversationToAI/ChangeOpportunityStage/MarkOpportunityWon/MarkOpportunityLost/ApproveAction/RejectAction/PauseOutbound/ResumeOutbound — clearer than generic updateStatus().

## 114-117. Generic updateStatus Banned + Testing Matrix + Critical Tests + Final Architecture
For important lifecycle entities ❌ updateStatus("whatever") bypasses business semantics; use explicit command methods. Testing matrix per transition: Valid FROM/Valid TO/Invalid FROM/Required fields/Permission/Policy/Suppression/Concurrency/Event emitted/History created. Critical tests: genuine reply prevents cold follow-up; Unsubscribe prevents all autonomous outreach; Approved stale email cannot execute after unsubscribe; Completed campaign cannot restart directly; AI cannot book meeting without real slot; Opportunity cannot become WON without required commercial state; Human takeover stops AI conversational sending; Emergency stop prevents queued outbound; Duplicate external action cannot execute twice; Knowledge conflict prevents unsafe commercial answer. Complete state architecture: WORKSPACE OUTBOUND (ACTIVE/PAUSED/STOP)→MARKET→DISCOVERY MISSION→COMPANY→CAMPAIGN→ENROLLMENT→CONVERSATION→(QUALIFICATION/OPPORTUNITY/MEETING/PROPOSAL/COMMITMENT); AI: GOAL→PLAN→MISSION→AGENT TASK; CONTROL: AI DECISION→POLICY→APPROVAL→EXTERNAL ACTION; INTELLIGENCE: OBSERVATION→FACT/SIGNAL→HYPOTHESIS→LEARNING. Each layer has own lifecycle.

## 118. Final Formula
REQUEST→CURRENT STATE→IS TRANSITION STRUCTURALLY VALID?→ACTOR AUTHORIZED?→REQUIRED DATA PRESENT?→CROSS-ENTITY INVARIANTS?→HARD SAFETY?→POLICY?→APPROVAL?→TRANSACTION→NEW STATE→STATE HISTORY→DOMAIN EVENT→AUDIT. Neither frontend nor AI can randomly change system state.
