# Technical Specification #2 — Master Database & Data Model (LOCKED)

## 1. Database Philosophy
PostgreSQL + Prisma ORM + pgvector. PostgreSQL = Canonical Business Truth. Redis/LLM/Provider/Vector = NOT truth. External data first becomes observation → evidence → canonical records.

## 2. 8 Logical Layers
01 Identity & Workspace / 02 Market & Discovery / 03 Company & People / 04 Intelligence & Evidence / 05 Outreach & Conversations / 06 Sales & Meetings / 07 AI Knowledge|Memory|Learning / 08 System|Policy|Audit. One physical PostgreSQL DB, logical separation. No microservices yet.

## 3. Global Rules
Common: id (UUID), workspace_id, created_at, updated_at, created_by, updated_by, archived_at, status, version. Timestamps UTC. Money: amount_minor + currency (never float). Display timezone conversion in frontend.

## 4-9. Identity & Workspace
Workspace (name/slug/default timezone/currency/language/status). User (email/name/status INVITED/ACTIVE/SUSPENDED/DISABLED/timezone/locale/last_active_at; historical users not hard-deleted). Role (OWNER/ADMIN/SALES/RESEARCHER/VIEWER). Permission (campaign.launch, opportunity.edit, pricing.approve, knowledge.publish, integration.manage). UserRole. AuthorityLimit (separate from permission: pricing.approve but max_discount_percent=10).

## 10-14. Market & Discovery
Market (name, country/state/city, industry, geographic_definition, status, discovery_mode). Market ≠ Search. DiscoveryMission (mode QUICK/DEEP/MARKET_EXHAUST/REFRESH/MONITOR, objective, budgets, coverage_confidence). DiscoveryQuery (query_type/text/provider/status/result_count/unique_company_count). DiscoveryObservation (provider record id, observed name/phone/address/domain, raw_payload_ref, observed_at) → then Entity Resolution.

## 15-21. Company
Company (legal_name/display_name/normalized_name/website_domain/phone/status/company_type/location/lat-lng/industry/employee_range/revenue_range/founded_year). Company status: DISCOVERED/RESEARCHING/ACTIVE/CUSTOMER/FORMER_CUSTOMER/DISQUALIFIED/ARCHIVED (not opportunity stage). ExternalEntityMapping (entity_type/entity_id/provider/external_id/external_url/first_seen/last_seen). CompanyAlias for dedupe. EntityMatchCandidate (confidence/matching+conflicting signals/status PENDING/AUTO_RESOLVED/MERGED/REJECTED/NEEDS_REVIEW). EntityMerge (source→target, merged_at/by/reason).

## 22-26. Person + Employment + Contact Point
Person (first/last/full name, status, linkedin/professional url, timezone, language; no permanent company_id/job_title). Employment (person_id, company_id, title, department, seniority, is_current, started/ended, confidence, verified_at). ContactPoint (entity_type/entity_id, type EMAIL/PHONE/MOBILE/BUSINESS_PHONE/OTHER, normalized_value, label, status, confidence, is_primary, first_seen/last_verified). ContactVerification (provider, status VALID/INVALID/RISKY/UNKNOWN/CATCH_ALL/TEMPORARY, verified_at, expires_at, provider_result). SocialProfile (platform, profile_url, handle, status, match_confidence, last_checked_at). Website (url, domain, status, is_official, match_confidence, first_seen/last_checked).

## 27-30. Website Intelligence
WebsiteSnapshot (website_id, captured_at, content_hash, title, metadata, storage_ref, audit_version). WebsiteAudit (status, performed_at, has_ssl/mobile_ready/contact_form/booking/chat/clear_cta, technology_summary, structured_findings, overall_confidence). Technology + CompanyTechnology.

## 31-40. Evidence, Fact, Signal, Hypothesis
Evidence (entity_type/entity_id, evidence_type, source_type/name/url/provider, observed_at, retrieved_at, content_excerpt/hash, storage_ref, confidence, freshness_status). Fact (entity_type/entity_id, fact_type, field, value_json, status, confidence, valid_from/until, first_observed/last_confirmed). FactEvidence many-to-many. Fact states: ACTIVE/CONFLICTED/SUPERSEDED/INVALIDATED (no blind overwrite). Signal (company_id|person_id, signal_type, title/description, detected_at/effective_at/expires_at, status, relevance, confidence); types HIRING/NEW_LOCATION/NEW_SERVICE/LEADERSHIP_CHANGE/WEBSITE_CHANGE/TECH_CHANGE/FUNDING/EXPANSION/CONTACT_LATER_DUE; SignalEvidence; state DETECTED/VALIDATED/ACTIONABLE/MONITOR/DISMISSED/EXPIRED/ACTED_ON. OpportunityHypothesis (company_id, service_id, hypothesis, reason_summary, status, confidence, generated_at, expires_at) + HypothesisEvidence.

## 41-45. ICP & Scoring & Services
ICP (multiple, name/description/status/version). ICPCriterion (field, operator, value_json, weight, required, criterion_type). Exclusions via criterion_type=EXCLUSION. ICPEvaluation (company_id, icp_id, icp_version, fit_score, status, evaluated_at, reason_summary). ScoreComponent (evaluation_id, dimension, score, weight, reason, evidence_refs) dimensions: ICP_FIT/OPPORTUNITY/INTENT/CONTACTABILITY/DATA_CONFIDENCE/PRIORITY. Service + Offer.

## 46-56. Campaign, Enrollment, Sequence, Conversation
Campaign (name, objective, market_id/icp_id/service_id/offer_id, channel, status, strategy_version, created_by, started/ended). Status: DRAFT/READY/ACTIVE/PAUSED/COMPLETED/ARCHIVED/BLOCKED. CampaignEnrollment (campaign_id, company_id, person_id, status, enrolled_at/removed_at, eligibility_snapshot, reason). Enrollment states: ELIGIBLE/ENROLLED/ACTIVE/REPLIED/PAUSED/COMPLETED/REMOVED/SUPPRESSED/BLOCKED. CompanyEngagementState (active_conversation_id/opportunity_id, last_outreach/reply, next_allowed_outreach_at, outreach_status). Sequence + SequenceStep (campaign_id, name, version, status; position/step_type/delay_seconds/template_id/conditions_json/status). Before each step: eligibility+conversation+policy re-evaluated.

## 57-62. Message + Classification + Extraction
MessageTemplate (channel, subject/body templates, version, status). Conversation (company_id, primary_person_id, channel, status, mode AUTO/ASSIST/HUMAN, campaign_id?, opportunity_id?, owner_user_id, last_message_at). Conversation status: COLD/CONTACTED/REPLIED/ENGAGED/QUALIFYING/QUALIFIED/MEETING_REQUESTED/MEETING_BOOKED/NURTURE/CLOSED/SUPPRESSED/ESCALATED. Message (conversation_id, direction, channel, sender_type/id, provider, provider_message_id/thread_id, subject/body, sent/received, status, campaign_step_id?; unique provider+provider_message_id). MessageClassification (classification, confidence, model_version, prompt_version, classified_at) classes POSITIVE/NEGATIVE/QUESTION/OBJECTION/REFERRAL/NOT_NOW/OUT_OF_OFFICE/UNSUBSCRIBE/WRONG_PERSON/PRICING/MEETING_REQUEST/AUTOMATED/UNKNOWN. ExtractionCandidate (source_type/source_id, entity_type/entity_id, field, proposed_value, confidence, status ACCEPTED/REJECTED/AUTO_ACCEPTED/NEEDS_REVIEW) — accepted values update structured records/memory.

## 63-72. Qualification, Opportunity, Pipeline, Proposal, Meeting
Qualification (company_id, conversation_id?, opportunity_id?, status, need/timeline/budget/authority/project_status, updated_at). QualificationAnswer (qualification_id, question_key, answer_json, confidence, source_message_id, evidence_id, verified). Opportunity (company_id, primary_person_id, name, pipeline_id, stage_id, service_id, offer_id, amount_minor, currency, status, owner_user_id, source_type/id, created/updated/closed_at). Multiple opportunities per company. Pipeline + PipelineStage (name, position, stage_type, is_closed/is_won/is_lost). OpportunityStageHistory (opportunity_id, from_stage_id, to_stage_id, changed_at, changed_by, reason) mandatory for duration/cycle/bottleneck analysis. OpportunityStakeholder (opportunity_id, person_id, role, influence_type, status). OpportunityLoss (opportunity_id, reason_code NO_BUDGET/NO_PRIORITY/NO_RESPONSE/TIMING/BAD_FIT/COMPETITOR/INTERNAL_DECISION/UNKNOWN, details, competitor_id?, lost_at). Proposal (opportunity_id, version, status, amount_minor, currency, valid_until, storage_ref, created_by, approved_by, created_at, sent_at) — versions never overwritten. Meeting (company_id, opportunity_id?, conversation_id?, meeting_type_id, owner_user_id, status, start_at/end_at, timezone, provider, provider_event_id, location_type, meeting_url). MeetingAttendee. MeetingBrief (meeting_id, version, summary, known_needs, open_questions, recent_activity, risk_notes, generated_at). MeetingOutcome (meeting_id, outcome_type, summary, next_step, recorded_at) outcomes: QUALIFIED/FOLLOW_UP/PROPOSAL_REQUESTED/NOT_NOW/NOT_FIT/NO_SHOW/CANCELLED.

## 73-80. Commitments, Tasks, Attention, Approvals
Commitment (company_id, person_id?, opportunity_id?, conversation_id?, direction WE_PROMISED/PROSPECT_PROMISED/MUTUAL, description, due_at, status OPEN/WAITING/DUE/COMPLETED/BROKEN/CANCELLED, owner_type/id, source_message_id?/meeting_id?) — different from Task. Task (type, title, description, status OPEN/IN_PROGRESS/WAITING/BLOCKED/COMPLETED/CANCELLED, priority, assigned_user_id/assigned_agent?, due_at, entity_type/id, source_type/id, created/completed). AttentionItem (attention_type, entity_type/entity_id, reason_code/summary, severity, status, created/resolved_at) powers Screen #14. ApprovalRequest (action_type, entity_type/id, requested_by_type/id, required_authority, action_payload, action_fingerprint, status PENDING/APPROVED/REJECTED/EXPIRED/INVALIDATED/CANCELLED, expires_at, created/resolved_at/resolved_by). Approval fingerprint covers material approved content/context — price change invalidates old approval.

## 81-90. Memory, Relationship, Knowledge
Memory (entity_type/entity_id, memory_type, key, value_json, status, confidence, valid_from/until, source_type/id, created/last_confirmed). Memory types: COMPANY/PERSON/CONVERSATION/RELATIONSHIP/OPPORTUNITY/COMMITMENT/PREFERENCE. MemoryEvidence. MemoryConflict (memory_a_id, memory_b_id, status, resolution). Relationship (from/to entity type/id, relationship_type, status, confidence, valid_from/until) types WORKS_AT/REFERRED_BY/PARTNER_OF/FORMER_EMPLOYEE_OF/RELATED_COMPANY. KnowledgeItem (type, title, status, scope_type/id, version, effective_from/until, created_by, approved_by). KnowledgeRevision (knowledge_item_id, version, content, structured_data, source_ref, created_at). KnowledgeStatus: DRAFT/IN_REVIEW/APPROVED/STALE/CONFLICT/ARCHIVED/REJECTED. Only approved/current in autonomous factual responses. Knowledge types: SERVICE/PRICING/FAQ/POLICY/INTEGRATION/CASE_STUDY/COMPETITOR/CLAIM/DOCUMENT/PROPOSAL_BLOCK. KnowledgeChunk (knowledge_revision_id, chunk_index, content, embedding vector) via pgvector. KnowledgeGap (question, category, occurrence_count, affected_opportunity_count, status, first/last seen) + KnowledgeGapOccurrence.

## 91-101. Goal, Mission, Agent, AI Run/Decision
Goal (name, objective, status, priority, owner_user_id, starts/ends, constraints_json, success_criteria_json). GoalMarket/GoalService/GoalICP relations + flexible constraints JSON. Mission (goal_id, mission_type, name, objective, status, priority, owner_type/id, budget, started/completed). MissionDependency. AgentTask (mission_id, agent_type, task_type, status, priority, input_json, output_json, started/completed, model_run_id?). AgentDefinition (agent_type, enabled, status, config_version) agents: MARKET_HUNTER/RESEARCH/CONTACT/SIGNAL/WEB_AUDIT/SCORING/CAMPAIGN/CONVERSATION/QUALIFICATION/SCHEDULING/REVENUE_INTELLIGENCE/LEARNING. AIRun (agent_type, model_provider/name, prompt_version, toolset_version, input_reference, output_reference, status, in/out tokens, cost_minor, started/completed). No private CoT. AIDecision (agent_type, action_type, entity_type/entity_id, decision PROPOSE/ACT/ASK/WAIT/BLOCK/ESCALATE, confidence, risk, reason_summary, policy_decision_id, created_at).

## 102-109. Policy, Suppression, Experiment, Learning
Policy (name, policy_type, scope_type/id, status, version, priority, effective_from/until). PolicyRule (policy_id, rule_type, condition_json, effect ALLOW/REQUIRE_APPROVAL/WAIT/BLOCK/ESCALATE, is_hard_rule, priority). PolicyRevision (policy_id, version, definition_json, created_by, approved_by, created_at). PolicyDecision (action_type, actor_type/id, entity_type/entity_id, decision, risk, confidence, matched_rules, reason_summary, policy_versions, evaluated_at). Suppression (scope_type, email/phone/person_id/company_id/domain, reason, source, status, created_at) reasons: UNSUBSCRIBE/DO_NOT_CONTACT/COMPLAINT/INVALID/LEGAL/MANUAL. Suppression survives deletion. Experiment (name, hypothesis, type, status, risk_level, primary_metric, guardrails_json, started/ended). ExperimentVariant (experiment_id, name, configuration_json, is_control). ExperimentAssignment (experiment_id, variant_id, entity_type/entity_id, assigned_at). ExperimentOutcome (experiment_id, variant_id, metric, value, sample_size, measured_at). Learning (learning_type, scope_type/scope_id, statement, status, confidence, source_experiment_id?, created_at, last_validated_at) + LearningEvidence.

## 110-120. Integration + Provider
Integration (provider_type, provider_name, status, capabilities, health_status, connected_at, last_sync_at). ProviderAccount (external_account_id, display_name, email, status, capabilities). ProviderCredential (credential_type, encrypted_payload, expires_at, updated_at). ProviderHealthCheck (status, latency_ms, error_code, checked_at). ProviderUsage (operation_type, units, cost_minor, currency, occurred_at).

## 121-132. External Action, Events, Audit, Activity, Notification
ExternalAction (action_type, entity_type/entity_id, status, idempotency_key UNIQUE, policy_decision_id, approval_request_id?, provider, provider_account_id, request_ref, response_ref, attempt_count, created_at, executed_at). States: PREPARED/APPROVED/QUEUED/EXECUTING/SUCCEEDED/FAILED/BLOCKED/CANCELLED. Crucial for retry safety (worker crash after provider accepted email → detect existing action, no resend). DomainEvent + OutboxEvent (event_type/version, aggregate_type/aggregate_id, payload, status, attempt_count, created_at, published_at) created in same transaction as mutation. InboundEvent (provider, external_event_id, event_type, payload_ref, status, received_at, processed_at; unique provider+external_event_id). AuditLog (actor_type/actor_id, action, entity_type/entity_id, before_json, after_json, reason, correlation_id, created_at) append-only. Activity (entity_type/entity_id, activity_type, summary, actor_type/actor_id, source_type/source_id, occurred_at) business timeline. Notification (user_id, type, severity, title, body, entity_type/entity_id, status, created/read_at).

## 133-146. Guardrails: Attribution, Analytics, Data Freshness, Confidence, No JSON Dump, DB Safety, Security/Retention/CoT
AttributionTouch (opportunity_id, touch_type, source_type/source_id, occurred_at, position). RevenueEvent (opportunity_id, type, amount_minor, currency, occurred_at). Analytics: DailyCampaignMetric / DailyMarketMetric / DailyPipelineMetric / DailyConversationMetrics derived, not source of truth. CommandCenterSnapshot cache/projection for #1. Confidence: normalized LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH + optional numeric, distinguish from truth. Status enums per entity: CampaignStatus/ConversationStatus/OpportunityStatus/TaskStatus/ApprovalStatus/SignalStatus/MissionStatus. JSONB only for flexible genuine cases; never huge nested blobs. DB constraints: FK/uniques/NOT NULL/check/transactions. Sensitive data classification: PUBLIC_BUSINESS/INTERNAL/CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED — Context Builder respects. Retention per class; audit longer than debug logs. No private chain-of-thought stored.

## 147-156. Relationships Map
Core model (WORKSPACE→USER/MARKET/ICP; MARKET→DiscoveryMission→Observations→COMPANY; COMPANY→People/Evidence/Signals; PERSON→Employment→Contact Points; +Conversations→Opportunities; star extensions to Meetings/Proposals/Commitments/Tasks). Outreach chain: Market→Company→ICP Evaluation→Enrollment→Sequence→Message→Conversation; reply→campaign automation STOP→Conversation Engine. Sales: Conversation→Qualification→Opportunity→Meeting→Proposal→Won/Lost. Intelligence: Evidence→Fact/Signal/Memory/Hypothesis/Learning. AI: Goal→Mission→AgentTask→AIRun→AIDecision→PolicyDecision→ExternalAction→DomainEvent→Outcome. Human Approval: AI Decision→Proposed External Action→Policy→ASK→Approval Request→Human→Approved→Revalidate→External Action. Revalidation before execution (10AM approval, 1PM unsubscribe → 2PM BLOCK). Account-level outreach protection before send: person/company/domain suppression, existing conversation/opportunity/reply/outreach/campaign conflict/validity. Revenue chain preserved.

## 157-168. Most Important Separations
Never mix: FACT / SIGNAL / HYPOTHESIS / MEMORY / KNOWLEDGE / LEARNING. Immutable rules. Build order: FOUNDATION (Workspace/User/Role/Permission)→CRM CORE (Company/Person/Employment/ContactPoint/ExternalEntityMapping)→DISCOVERY (Market/Mission/Observation/Evidence/Fact)→INTELLIGENCE (Website/Audit/Signal/ICP/Evaluation/Hypothesis)→OUTREACH (Campaign/Enrollment/Sequence)→CONVERSATION (Conversation/Message/Classification)→SALES (Qualification/Opportunity/Pipeline/Meeting/Proposal)→WORK (Commitment/Task/Attention/Approval)→AI (Goal/Mission/AgentTask/AIRun/Decision)→MEMORY/KB (Memory/Relationship/Knowledge)→CONTROL (Policy/Suppression/ExternalAction)→SYSTEM (Events/Outbox/Inbox/Audit)→LEARNING/ANALYTICS (Experiments/Learning/Attribution/Projections).

## Example: Austin Landscaper End-to-End
GreenScape: Market→Mission→Observation→Entity Resolution→Company→Evidence→WebsiteAudit→Fact(no booking form)→Signal(commercial expansion)→OpportunityHypothesis→ICP Evaluation→Person(John Smith)→Employment(Owner)→ContactPoint→Verification→CampaignEnrollment→Message→Conversation→Positive Reply→Qualification→Meeting→Opportunity→Proposal→WON→RevenueEvent. Full chain traceable.

## Deliberate Non-Goals
no giant leads table, no company+person same record, no provider JSON as CRM, no one generic status, no one AI score without reasons, no company.status=deal stage, no raw AI output directly mutating truth, no hard deleting sales history, no Redis as permanent state, no everything-in-JSON, no AI facts without provenance, no approvals without version/fingerprint, no email retries without idempotency, no private CoT storage.

## Database Build Order
Foundation → CRM Core → Discovery → Intelligence → Outreach → Conversation → Sales → Work → AI → Memory/KB → Control → System → Learning/Analytics.

## Locked Philosophy
> External data becomes observations. Observations create evidence. Evidence supports facts/signals. AI creates hypotheses and decisions. Only controlled domain workflows modify canonical business state.
