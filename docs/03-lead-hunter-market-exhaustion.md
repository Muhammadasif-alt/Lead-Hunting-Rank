# 03 — Lead Hunter: Geo Market Exhaustion + Local Business Intelligence

## Core idea
User command deta: "Texas → Austin ke tamam landscapers find karo. Website ho ya na ho. Maximum possible businesses cover karo."
Objective: multiple permitted/public sources ko merge + deduplicate karke city-level market map banana. Sirf Google top-20 nahi.

## Market coverage result (example)
- Businesses discovered / unique / with website / without website / phone / email / Facebook / Instagram / owner found / verified email — coverage score + source provenance ke saath (100% guarantee nahi; coverage honest dikhana).

## Business 360° profile (per business)
- BUSINESS: category, address, phone, website, Google profile, rating, reviews
- ONLINE PRESENCE: website ✓/✕, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, other profiles
- DECISION MAKERS: owner, role, business email, public business phone, professional details
- BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE: services, website quality, mobile friendly, booking system, online chat, review response, social activity
- AI OPPORTUNITIES: website redesign / AI chat / lead automation / review automation / SEO opportunity — HIGH/MED/LOW

## No-website = opportunity signal
Example: Texas Lawn Masters — no website but Google profile + FB + IG + phone + owner. AI SIGNAL: 🔥 No Website → possible opportunity: website + online quote/booking.

## Market Exhaust Mode
Quick Search / Deep Search / Exhaust Market. Sources searched n/8, queries exhausted %, duplication resolution. Estimated Coverage: HIGH.
Territory define: country/state/city/radius/industry + include website/no-website/small/multi-location + enrich details (website, social, decision makers, contact data, services, reviews/signals, tech, AI opportunity).

## Natural-language filters
- Austin landscapers without website
- Website hai lekin booking/chat nahi
- Owner identified + verified email
- 50+ reviews, weak website
- Facebook active magar website nahi

## Pipeline
Market Discovery → Business Resolution/Dedup → Website+Social Discovery → Owner/Decision Maker Discovery → Contact Enrichment + Verification → Digital Presence Audit → AI Opportunity Detection → ICP Scoring → Prioritization → Outreach → Conversation → Meeting

## Rule: every fact stores source + last checked date
Facts = evidence; stale ya inference alag. Autonomous outreach safer/smarter banta.

## Scope
Same framework: landscapers, plumbers, roofers, HVAC, dentists, contractors, salons, law firms, real-estate, etc.
