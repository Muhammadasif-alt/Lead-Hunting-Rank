# Screen #01 — Command Center / Dashboard

## 1. Purpose
Normal CRM dashboard nahi. Autonomous Revenue OS ka Mission Control. User 30 sec mein samjhe: aaj sales mein kya ho raha, AI ne kya kiya, kahan human zarurat, hot prospects, markets, conversations, meetings, pipeline, risks, AI next kya.
Core: UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → INTERVENE ONLY WHERE NEEDED.

## 2. Structure
Header (logo, Command Center, nav sidebar, AI status, user). Welcome header → AI Command Bar → TODAY AT A GLANCE → AI Briefing → Human Attention + Live AI Activity → Hot Prospects + Active Markets → Pipeline + Buying Signals → Meetings + Campaign Performance. Modular cards, random widget grid nahi.

## 3. Global Header
COMMAND CENTER, AI STATUS always visible: ● ACTIVE / ASSIST / PARTIALLY PAUSED / OUTBOUND PAUSED / DEGRADED / HUMAN CONTROL / EMERGENCY STOP. [Pause Outreach], Notifications, User.

## 4. Emergency Status
Outbound paused → prominent banner: reason, paused by, timestamp, [View Details]. Inbound continues.

## 5. Welcome / Context Header
"Good morning. Your AI sales team is active. 3 items need attention. 2 meetings today. 17 high-priority prospects overnight." No generic "welcome back!".

## 6. Global AI Command Bar
"Ask AI Sales Manager anything..." — system command interface, simple chatbot nahi. Find landscapers, companies with no website, prospects needing attention, why AI stopped GreenScape conversation, opportunities >$20K, campaign generated most qualified, prepare meetings, pause Austin outreach, positive replies this week.

## 7. Command Understanding
Command → intent detection → entity/scope resolution → permission → read or action → policy → execute/preview/approval → result. High-impact silently execute nahi. "4,200 ko email" → action preview first.

## 8. Today at a Glance
KPI strip: New Prospects, High Intent, Conversations, Meetings, Open Pipeline, Human Needed. Each with sub-metric.

## 9. Metrics Must Be Actionable
Primary: Qualified Prospects, Positive Conversations, Meetings, Qualified Opportunities, Pipeline Value, Revenue, Human Attention. Vanity avoided: emails sent, opens, page views, AI tokens.

## 10. KPI Drilldown
18 HIGH INTENT → filtered list. Human Needed → #14. Navigation through business state.

## 11. AI Sales Manager Briefing
Since your last review: discovered/matched/replies/requests/meetings/pricing approval. Main bottleneck (12 qualified waiting for contact verification). Recommended focus. [Open Full Briefing] [Ask AI]. Screen #2 condensed.

## 12. Briefing Must Be Evidence-Based
No "sales going great". X→Y change. Drilldown available.

## 13. Briefing Priority
1 Urgent human decisions → 2 Revenue opportunities → 3 Relationship risks → 4 Meetings → 5 Campaign issues → 6 Market discoveries → 7 General.

## 14. Human Attention Queue
🔴 GreenScape custom pricing $18K 42min → [Review]. 🟠 Bright Lawn unsupported technical question. 🟡 Campaign cohort expansion 75→200. View All link. Connected to #14.

## 15. Why Human?
Every item: "WHY HUMAN?" — custom pricing exceeds AI authority / KB no approved answer / confidence below threshold.

## 16. Attention Priority
Not newest-first. Urgency, revenue impact, wait time, stage, deadline, relationship risk, approval expiry, meeting proximity, severity.

## 17. Quick Actions
Review/Approve/Edit/Take Over/Assign/Snooze. High-risk approval needs context first.

## 18. Live Autonomous Activity
Business-meaningful: research started, 14 new found, owner email verified, reply classified, discovery call booked, account conflict skipped. Click → AI ACTION card: action, company, reason, policy, result, Open Company.

## 19. Activity Must Show Important Actions
No "parsed JSON / called database / generated embedding" — technical logs.

## 20. Active Markets
Per market: companies found, contactable, high priority, coverage confidence, new this week, status, Open Market. Status: DISCOVERING/ENRICHING/MONITORING/SATURATED/PAUSED/NEEDS ATTENTION.

## 21. Coverage
Never "100% of Austin found". Estimated Coverage HIGH, strategies exhausted 92%, sources 8/8, methodology accessible.

## 22. Market Opportunity Summary
High-priority count + breakdown: no verified website, weak digital conversion, buying/growth signals, verified decision-maker contacts. Click → Lead Hunter.

## 23. Hot Prospects
Card list: company, city, owner identified, high ICP, recent signal, active conversation, NEXT action.

## 24. Why Hot?
Never unexplained 98/100. WHY PRIORITIZED: strong ICP fit, verified decision maker, positive reply, recent expansion signal, relevant opportunity.

## 25. Opportunity Hypothesis
WHY NOW? Company expanded commercial offering, website has no quote flow → evidence. Facts/inferences separated.

## 26. Pipeline Snapshot
Stage + count + value: New/Engaged/Qualified/Meeting/Proposal/Negotiation + Won/Lost month. Click → #7.

## 27. Pipeline Risk
3 opportunities 7+ days no activity. 2 proposals awaiting response. 1 commitment due today. Exceptions only.

## 28. Opportunity Momentum
Deal name, stage, recent activity, next commitment, human required YES. No fake "85% likely to close" without transparent methodology.

## 29. Meetings Today
Time, company, type, opportunity, attendees, [Open Brief].

## 30. Meeting Readiness
READY / NEEDS REVIEW. Brief ready, research refreshed, conversation summarized, open questions identified. Expandable: WHY THEY'RE MEETING, KNOWN NEED, IMPORTANT CONTEXT, OPEN QUESTIONS.

## 31. Buying Signals
Signal, company, detected time, relevance High/Med. Signal ≠ opportunity. Fields: SIGNAL / AI RELEVANCE / NEXT ACTION (Research/Monitor/Contact).

## 32. Campaign Performance
Per campaign: active prospects, positive replies, qualified, meetings, opportunities, pipeline, status Healthy. Primary = downstream outcomes.

## 33. Campaign Alerts
⚠ verification failure rate increased → AI reduced enrollment speed. Cohort completed → recommend next cohort.

## 34. Deliverability Snapshot
Healthy → compact. Unhealthy → prominent. Mailboxes/bounce/provider.

## 35. Conversation Snapshot
New replies, positive/questions/meeting request/not now/wrong person. Human needed count. Important conversation card: latest message, AI classification, current mode, reason, Open Conversation.

## 36. Commitments Due
Send GreenScape revised scope Owner Alex 3PM. Follow up with LawnPro due 4PM. Waiting on Prospect: 4 proposals/3 questions/2 confirmations. No fake tasks while prospect expected to act.

## 37. AI Recommendations
Highest-value: expand cohort 50→100 with evidence, positive replies, qualified, deliverability healthy. Categories: Market/Targeting/Campaign/Conversation/Pipeline/Knowledge Gap/Data Quality/Deliverability/Experiment. Lifecycle: PROPOSED→REVIEWED→APPROVED/REJECTED→EXECUTED→OUTCOME MEASURED.

## 38. AI Knowledge Gaps
3 prospects asked ServiceTitan, no KB answer. Impact: 2 active opportunities, 1 new conversation. [Answer Once] [Open KB].

## 39. Data Quality Alert
27 high-priority missing verified decision-maker → [Run Enrichment].

## 40. Integration Health
Exceptions: Gmail healthy, Calendar healthy, Verification provider degraded → delayed jobs. Full details #15/#18.

## 41. Goal Progress
Current goal, period, full funnel: discovered→contactable→engaged→qualified→meetings→opportunities→won. Factual states: ON TRACK/NEEDS ATTENTION/BLOCKED/INSUFFICIENT DATA against explicitly defined targets.

## 42. Daily Plan
TODAY'S AI PLAN items 1..n. Per item: Pause/Inspect/Change Priority where authorized. Resource use: research jobs, enrichments, verified contacts, outbound sends X/limit, AI spend $, data spend $. Budget warning at 85%.

## 43. Global Search / Recent Items
Search companies/people/conversations/opportunities/campaigns grouped results. Recent items in search menu.

## 44. Notifications Philosophy
Notify: decision needed, approval, commitment at risk, high-value inbound, meeting issue, system incident, security event. NOT: lead discovered, email opened, research completed, score changed.

## 45. Role-Aware
Owner: revenue/pipeline/goals/AI status/approvals/risks/health. Sales: conversations/meetings/opportunities/commitments/attention. Researcher: markets/data quality/research/resolution/evidence. Viewer: read-only. MY WORK / WORKSPACE toggle.

## 46. Time Range + Comparison
Today/7d/30d/Month/Custom, but meetings remain today. Raw counts + change; no misleading % on tiny samples.

## 47. Empty State / First-Time
NO ACTIVE MARKET → example prompt → [Start With AI]. Setup checklist for new workspace: Business Brain, email, calendar, ICP, first market discovery.

## 48. Loading / Error States
Independent card loading. Error: show last successful update + retry. Never fake zeros. Freshness labels. Live updates via SSE.

## 49. Lead Hunter Progress + Mission Card
Market Exhaust live: found, new this round, sources, saturation. Mission card: goal → steps discovery/resolution/audit/contacts/scoring with statuses. Better than generic %.

## 50. Since Last Visit + What Changed
AI completed: +143 discovered, +52 verified, +17 high-priority, +8 replies, +2 meetings, +1 escalation. Button "What changed since yesterday?" across markets/campaigns/conversations/pipeline/meetings/signals/risks. Evidence-backed.

## 51. Ask Why + Ask What Next
Every major item: Why is GreenScape high priority? Why did AI pause? Why flagged? Why does this need me? [What should happen next?] → Next Best Action + reason.

## 52. Quick Create
+ menu: New Market/Company/Opportunity/Campaign/Task. NL command primary.

## 53. No Separate Notification Screen / No Contacts Dashboard
High-value notifications in Command Center, #14, record. Contacts inside Company 360 (#04).

## 54. Card Customization
Limited show/hide + reorder of secondary cards. Core standardized. Visual hierarchy per locked direction: near-black green top, deep emerald middle, luminous mint accent, dark translucent cards, subtle borders, soft glow.

## 55. Colors + Density
Green healthy, amber attention/degraded, red critical/incident, neutral info. Priority stack: Command → Human Attention → AI Briefing → Today's Business → Markets/Pipeline/Conversations → Secondary Performance.

## 56. Backend Architecture
No duplicate business logic. Consumes summaries: Lead Hunter→markets, Signals→signals, Conversations→intent/human, Calendar→meetings, Opportunities→pipeline, Campaigns→campaign, Tasks→attention, AI Manager→briefing/recommendations, Analytics→revenue/goals. Aggregate read model `CommandCenterSnapshot` (today/aiBriefing/attention/hotProspects/meetings/markets/pipeline/signals/campaigns/goals/systemHealth) + live events.

## 57. Performance
Precomputed read models + transactional data; AI briefing cached/on-demand on meaningful changes. Hot prospects from structured prioritization: ICP fit + opportunity relevance + intent signals + relationship + contactability + recency + commercial context → explainable reasons. Human attention: event → can AI resolve? yes→AI, no→is human actually required? wait→WAIT, yes→Attention Queue.

## 58. Security + Audit + Safety
Role/permissions/scope/assignments/authority respected; AI command bar not permission bypass ("export every contact" → not authorized). Actions audited: Approve/Reject/Take Over/Pause Campaign/Pause Outreach/Change AI mode/Execute AI command. Global safety: AI status + autonomy L3, [Pause New Outreach], [Pause AI Replies], [Open AI Control Center], [STOP ALL OUTBOUND]. Pause Outreach stops new automated prospecting sends; STOP ALL OUTBOUND stops automated external communication; inbound processing continues.

## 59. Success Criterion + Final Flow
Morning check answers: What happened? What matters? What needs me? What's AI doing? What's at risk? What's next? User opens → Command Center → Understanding/human attention → markets/pipeline/meetings → why now → next best action → AI or human → outcome → learning.

## 60. Definition
Command Center = Command Center + Morning Briefing + Human Decision Queue + AI Activity Monitor + Revenue Overview + NL Control Surface. "Tell AI what you want → Review what matters → Take over only when necessary."
