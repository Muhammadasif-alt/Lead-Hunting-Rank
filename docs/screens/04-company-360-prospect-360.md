# Screen #4: Company 360° + Prospect 360°

Company = primary object. Prospects = people inside company. Company context aani chahiye har agent ko.

## 1. Company 360° Header
← back to market. Business name + HOT priority, industry/city/status, website/phone/email/FB/IG. 4 score blocks: ICP FIT / OPPORTUNITY / INTENT / DATA QUALITY. AI status (Prospecting). NEXT BEST ACTION. Actions: Start Outreach / Add to Campaign / Research.

## 2. Tabs
Overview / People / Digital Presence / Intelligence / Conversations / Opportunities / Activity / Memory.

## 3. Overview Tab
Left: Business details (name, industry, services, location, service area, phone, website, hours, status). Right: AI Company Summary with primary opportunity + confidence. Auto-refresh on material change.

## 4. Why This Prospect?
Territory/industry/active/owner/contactable/reviews/opportunity reasons. AI priority HIGH.

## 5. Opportunity Map
Per-opportunity bars: Website Redesign/AI Chat/Online Booking/Lead Automation/Review Automation/Local SEO/CRM/Missed Call. Click → OBSERVED + INFERENCE + Confidence + evidence chain. Fact≠inference.

## 6. People Tab
People cards: name, role, decision power, contactability, relationship, email status, phone, LinkedIn, Open Prospect. AI recommended contact + why.

## 7. Prospect 360°
Name, role, company, relationship status. Email/status/phone/profile. CURRENT INTENT, QUALIFICATION x/5, LAST CONTACT, NEXT ACTION. Open Conversation.

## 8. Prospect Intelligence
Professional context only: role, decision authority, company, interests/signals, communication preference, observed response behavior (from our own interaction data). No unnecessary personal profiling.

## 9. Digital Presence Tab
Website (status/mobile/SSL/form/booking/chat), Facebook (matched/activity/followers/last activity), Instagram, Business listings.

## 10. Website Visual History
Snapshots over time. AI detects changes: new service page → potential signal → future outreach context.

## 11. Technology Intelligence
CMS/Analytics/Booking/Chat/CRM/Call Tracking/Forms. Unknown = Unknown, no AI guess.

## 12. Review Intelligence
Rating, reviews, recent velocity, owner response rate, common positive/negative themes. Possible opportunity (internal only, not in outreach automatically).

## 13. Intelligence Tab
FACTS / SIGNALS / AI HYPOTHESES with confidences. Never mixed.

## 14. Evidence Drawer
Fact → confidence + evidence list (source + last checked). Conflicting sources → Needs verification.

## 15. Conversations Tab
All contacts across channels: name, channel, intent, last reply. Active conversation protects company; no unrelated cold campaign to other employees.

## 16. Conversation Summary
Initial outreach → reply → AI answered approved info → current status → important → next best action.

## 17. Qualification Card
Need ✓ / Authority ✓ / Timeline ? / Budget ? / Fit ✓ / Overall x/5 known. AI collects missing info naturally.

## 18. Relationship Memory
Dated memory: busy until Sept → eligible → positive reply → asked pricing. Key memory: prefers email, interested in lead automation, asked about website+AI chat, no budget confirmed, no pricing commitment.

## 19. Promises & Commitments
OUR SIDE (portfolio sent, follow-up Friday) + PROSPECT SIDE. Due → Next Best Action engine.

## 20. Next Best Action
WAIT / HUMAN CALL + reason + recommended review + confidence.

## 21. AI Strategy Panel
[Ask AI] side panel: recommended strategy, primary person, angle, avoid, evidence, suggested CTA, confidence.

## 22. Company Chat
NL queries: sab kuch, pehle kya send, owner, why high priority, promises pending, safe to contact, website issues, last 6 months changes. AI answers only company context.

## 23. Opportunity Tab
One company, multiple opportunities (Website Conversion, AI Lead Automation, Local SEO). Company ≠ Opportunity.

## 24. Opportunity Timeline
Event timeline with dates.

## 25. Activity Timeline
Everything with filters: All/AI Actions/Emails/Research/Signals/Human/System.

## 26. AI Action Audit
Action → WHY → RULE → CONFIDENCE.

## 27. Data Freshness
Per field last checked; intelligently refresh stale before important outreach.

## 28. Pre-Outreach Revalidation
Business active? / website unchanged? / owner valid? / email valid? / contacted? / suppressed? / active conversation? / opportunity valid? Website changed → refresh personalization.

## 29. Account-Level Contact Protection
Active negotiation → do not cold-contact other employees without approval.

## 30. Contact History Across Companies
John moved company → previous relationship recognized; new company context independently verified.

## 31. Internal Team Notes
Humans add notes + @mentions. AI can use if allowed.

## 32. AI Memory vs Human Notes
FACT / AI MEMORY / HUMAN NOTE / AI INFERENCE — clearly distinct.

## 33. Risk / Restrictions Panel
Email/SMS/Phone allowed, suppression, unsubscribe. DO NOT CONTACT lock — AI cannot override.

## 34. Prospect Temperature
🔥 HIGH evidence-based: replied twice, asked pricing, meeting CTA, recent engagement.

## 35. Similar Companies
Accounts resembling this one.

## 36. "Clone Winning Customer"
Won deal → extract industry/size/location/services/digital gaps/persona/signals/journey → Lead Hunter searches similar.

## 37. Revenue Learning
WHY DID THIS DEAL CLOSE? initial signal/persona/angle/first reply/sales cycle/final product → AI Sales Manager.

## 38. Header answers 7 questions
Who, good fit?, who to speak, why need us, relationship, what recently, what next.

## Final structure
Smart Header → AI Summary → Next Best Action → Overview → People → Prospect 360 → Digital Presence (Website/Social/Tech/Reviews) → Intelligence (Facts/Signals/Hypotheses/Evidence) → Conversations → Qualification → Opportunities → Relationship Memory → Promises → Activity → Team Notes → Audit/Restrictions.
