# Screen #17 — AI Control Center + Autonomy & Policies

## Purpose
Authority and safety brain. Baaki screens intelligence/actions deti, ye decide kare: kya kar sakta, kitna, kis condition mein, kab human.

## 1. Autonomy Levels
L0 MANUAL (AI analyze/recommend/draft, human executes) / L1 ASSISTED / L2 SEMI-AUTONOMOUS (research/enrich/score/routine/approved FU, human first outreach+sensitive) / L3 AUTONOMOUS SALES (discover/research/prioritize/outreach/FU/routine convos/qualify/schedule; human exceptions/pricing/proposals/sensitive) / L4 GOAL DRIVEN (goal+boundaries → AI plans/executes within policy; human supervises high-risk).

## 2. Global Level = Preset
Global level starting preset only. Actual: Global → Agent → Action → Campaign/Market → Account restriction → Current context. Most restrictive wins.

## 3. Action Decision Model
AI WANTS TO ACT → is action permitted? → entity eligible? → suppression? → enough evidence? → confidence? → within authority? → within budget? → within limits? → human approval? → ACT/ASK/WAIT/BLOCK.

## 4-7. ACT/ASK/WAIT/BLOCK
ACT: research allowed, budget available, low risk. ASK HUMAN: custom pricing, no authority. WAIT: "contact me next month". BLOCK: unsubscribed, even with bypass.

## 8-18. Agent Control + Agent Detail
Market Hunter/Research/Contact/Signal/Web Audit/Scoring/Campaign/Conversation/Qualification/Scheduling/Revenue Intelligence/Learning ON/OFF. Per agent: status/autonomy/CAN/REQUIRED HUMAN. Market Hunter: search/dedupe/resolve/website discovery, limits on markets/businesses/budget/deep search. Research: basic+deep auto for high-priority, expensive budgeted, unsupported private blocked. Contact: find/verify auto; risky do-not-send, invalid blocked, uncertain review. Signal: monitor/dedupe/validate/hypothesis auto; outreach from raw signal NO. Campaign: strategy/drafts/priority/variants/timing auto; launch policy controlled; change offer/price/compliance blocked/human. Conversation: interested auto, grounded FAQ auto, approved objection auto, meeting request auto; custom pricing/contract/legal/complaint/unknown human. Qualification: extract/ask/update auto; invent budget/authority/timeline blocked. Scheduling: availability/slots/book auto; outside hours blocked; no invented slot. Learning: observe/patterns/hypotheses/design experiment auto; run experiment policy controlled; rewrite prod rules NO; high-impact human.

## 19. Authority Matrix
Auto: research/enrichment/verification/score/routine first outreach*/routine FU*/FAQ reply*/book approved meeting/standard pricing*/Auto-scheduling. Approval: custom quote/discount/proposal. Block: contract modification/override unsubscribe/invent unsupported claim. *=subject to policy/context.

## 20-22. Confidence Policies
Classification internal routing medium OK; pricing stronger evidence; outbound claim must be grounded; entity merge high or human review. Confidence ≠ Permission (100% + custom discount no authority→human). Permission ≠ Confidence (authority but weak KB match→human).

## 23-24. Evidence Requirement + Grounding
Claim "You don't have a website" requires official search + fresh verification + confidence. Outbound factual claims grounded in Approved KB/fresh company facts/direct conversation/verified opportunity data/approved case studies. AI inference strategy nahi, fact nahi.

## 25-27. Escalation Policies
ESCALATE WHEN: low confidence/pricing exception/discount/contract-legal/sensitive complaint/explicit human request/unsupported capability/large opportunity threshold/conflicting critical data/unusual commitment/policy ambiguity. < $10K normal, $10-25K more review, > $25K human involvement for selected commercial actions. New prospect vs active opportunity vs customer vs negotiation → different policy.

## 28-31. Account/Conversation/Campaign/Market Autonomy
Company 360 override (GreenScape = Human Led). Conversation: AUTO/ASSIST/HUMAN. Campaign: controlled, may prioritize/personalize/send/FU, may NOT change offer/increase limits/expand territory. Market: Austin discovery autonomous, continuous monitoring, auto-enroll with AI validation, outreach campaign policy.

## 32. Policy Precedence
HARD SYSTEM RULE → workspace policy → market → campaign → company → conversation → action. Hard restrictions can't be loosened lower.

## 33-35. Outreach/Frequency/Account Protection
Max new contacts/day, per mailbox, max FU/contact, min gap, global frequency cap, working hours, allowed days. Same person across all campaigns max. Account: John active engaged → Sarah cold sequence blocked.

## 36-37. Follow-up Limits + Working Hours
Max FU 3; genuine reply → 0 campaign FU, Conversation Engine owns. Outreach window prospect local time. Timezone unknown → research/company location/wait per policy.

## 38-41. Spend Control
Daily/monthly: AI/Research/Enrichment/Verification/LLM. Hierarchy: workspace→market→campaign→mission. Threshold: 70% normal→85% notify→95% reduce background→100% stop non-critical. Never silently exceed cap for high-value work; → approve additional spend.

## 42. Cost Per Mission
Market mission: estimate discovery/enrichment/verification/AI before execution + budget cap.

## 43-46. Lead Hunter Limits + Saturation + Campaign Scaling + Scale Gate
Market exhaust: max rounds/source budget/max expensive enrichment/runtime/saturation stop. Search saturation stop when diminishing. Validation 25→expansion 75→scale 200+. Scale gate: data quality/deliverability/policy/negative response/sample sufficient → hold otherwise.

## 47-50. Deliverability/Unsubscribe/Invalid/Duplicate Guardrails
Bounce threshold→stop new sends, continue inbound, continue safe convos, reverify, alert admin. Unsubscribe: immediate suppression, cancel FU, audit, no AI decision. Invalid→block. Risky/unknown→policy. Duplicate: person received?/another campaign?/company engaged?/opportunity?/human convo? → skip/resolve.

## 51-55. Pricing/Proposal/Contract/Promise Authority
AI can explain approved pricing/quote approved fixed/calculate deterministic via pricing engine, can't custom/discount/payment exception. Custom discount→human. Proposal: draft with approved blocks/case studies/requirements; final approve/price change/unsupported commitment/legal terms→human. Contractâ€™: indemnity/liability/warranty/custom obligation→human. Promise: "Friday tak" → check authorized/timeline approved.

## 56-59. Capability/Sensitive Complaint/Human Request/Uncertain Identity
XYZ integration without KB→need confirmation→escalate; no "absolutely". Sensitive complaint→stop persuasion, preserve evidence, human. "Can I speak with a person?" → immediate human. Uncertain identity→resolve or human review, don't update random CRM.

## 60-61. Data Quality Gate + Freshness Policy
Company match/contact match/email validity/website freshness/opportunity state/suppression. Freshness: website short requirement, owner periodic, business email per policy, prospect statement persistent until contradicted.

## 62-65. AI Action Budget + Mission Budget + Geographic + Industry Boundaries
Counts/day: companies researched/deep research/contacts enriched/emails generated/sent/FUs/signal scans. Mission: max businesses/enrichment/verified contacts/initial outreach/spend. Geographic boundary Austin≠all Texas. Industry boundary Landscaping + controlled related (lawn care/irrigation/hardscaping); unrelated excluded.

## 66-68. Goal Constraints + Feasibility + Planner Approval
Goal has market/budget/channels/ICP/daily volume/services/authority/duration. Feasibility check: goal recorded + plan + constraints + no guaranteed outcome. Level 4 plan→review→execute; low-risk auto within policy configurable.

## 69-70. Strategy Boundaries + Experiment Boundaries
AI may optimize: send timing/research depth/prospect priority/approved message variation/CTA/FU timing. May NOT silently change: pricing/legal/suppression/compliance/global spend/global limits. Learning agent: observe/pattern/hypothesis/experiment auto; run low-risk policy controlled; adopt low-risk policy controlled; high-impact human.

## 71-74. Model Change Control + Shadow/Dry Run/Simulation
New model: evaluate→shadow test→compare→approve→limited rollout→monitor. Shadow AI processes same case, no action, compare classification/recommendation/draft/policy compliance/grounding. Dry run: research+scoring+decision+generation+policy checks, no external action. Simulation: Level 3 enable→what becomes autonomous/what remains human before applying.

## 75-78. Policy Change Preview + Versioning + Rollback + Effective Date
Changing conversation replies assist→auto shows affected active conversations 31, would allow grounded answers, would not allow pricing/legal/unsupported technical. Every policy version: who/when/why/what. Rollback creates new version based on v6. Effective date scheduling: new daily outreach limit effective Monday.

## 79-81. Policy Conflict + Explain Effective + Simulator
Global max 100/day, campaign wants 150 → conflict, effective max 100. [Why Is This Blocked?] → blocked by company policy Human Only, stricter than global. Policy simulator: GreenScape + pricing reaction + context negotiation/custom 8-location → DECISION ASK HUMAN, custom pricing + negotiation + no authority.

## 82-87. Granular Pause/Kill Switch + Confirmation
[PAUSE AI] / [PAUSE NEW OUTREACH] / [PAUSE FOLLOW-UPS] / [PAUSE AI REPLIES] / [STOP ALL OUTBOUND]. Kill switch blocks campaign sends/FUs/AI replies/automated meeting messages/other outbound; inbound continues. Confirmation prompt: 🔴 banner on every screen, paused by + time.

## 88-90. Resume Safety + Queued Action Expiry
Resume check: queued sends/data freshness/campaign validity/mailbox health/suppression changes/conversation replies received→rebuild eligibility. Queens have VALID UNTIL/revalidation before execution.

## 91. AI Incident Detection
Unexpected reply pattern/grounding failures/provider errors/high bounce/repeated human corrections/policy block spike → automatically reduce autonomy for affected scope (Conversation Agent auto→assist) + notify human.

## 92-94. Fail Safe / Fail Closed / Operational Continuity
When uncertain whether AI authorized: don't act, default ASK/WAIT. High-risk actions: policy engine unavailable→fail closed, no custom pricing/contract/send. But policy service issue doesn't stop reading CRM/viewing analytics/manual work/inbound storage.

## 95-99. Audit + Block + Override + Soft vs Hard
AI action audit: AGENT/WHY/EVIDENCE/POLICY/AUTHORITY/CONFIDENCE/RESULT. Block audit: action attempt + reason. Human override audit: recommendation/human action/user/time/result. Hard rule blocks even human attempt. SOFT: recommendation/preferred timing/suggested depth/default interval. HARD: suppression/invalid email/security/authority boundaries/compliance/global emergency.

## 100. Final Layout
Global control/current authority/agent health/limits+guardrails/escalation rules/pause buttons.

## 101. Ultimate Formula
CAN AI DO THIS? = CAPABILITY × PERMISSION × AUTONOMY POLICY × DATA CONFIDENCE × GROUNDING × BUSINESS RULES × RELATIONSHIP STATE × BUDGET × OPERATIONAL HEALTH → ACT/ASK/WAIT/BLOCK. Fail → NO UNSAFE ACTION.

## 102. Ultimate Loop
Human goal→AI plan→policy engine→AI executes allowed→each action revalidated→low risk auto/high risk human/hard restriction block/not yet appropriate wait→outcome→audit→analytics→learning→propose improvement→controlled experiment→approved policy/strategy update.
