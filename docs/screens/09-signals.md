## 1. Signal categories

Signals ko structured taxonomy mein rakhenge:

```text
BUSINESS GROWTH

• New location
• New office
• New service
• Market expansion
• New territory
• Hiring increase
• Team growth
• Funding where relevant
• Acquisition / merger


DIGITAL

• New website
• Major website redesign
• New service pages
• Booking system added/removed
• Chat added/removed
• Technology change
• New e-commerce capability
• Domain change


COMMERCIAL

• New contracts
• New projects
• Partnerships
• New customer segment
• New pricing/service structure


PEOPLE

• New owner where publicly established
• Leadership change
• Relevant executive hire
• Decision-maker role change


MARKETING

• Increased social activity
• New advertising/marketing activity where reliably observable
• New content campaign
• Review activity change


RELATIONSHIP

• "Contact me later" date reached
• Old prospect re-engaged
• Previous customer returns
• New referral
• Dormant conversation receives reply


INTERNAL SALES

• Proposal viewed/acknowledged where supported
• Prospect asks pricing
• Prospect requests meeting
• New stakeholder appears
• Opportunity becomes inactive
```

Not every category applies to every industry.

# 3. Signal ≠ Intent

Bahut important distinction lock karni hai.

Agar company ne website redesign ki:

```text
FACT
Website materially changed.
```

Ye automatically prove nahi karta:

```text
"They want our service."
```

System structure:

```text
OBSERVED EVENT
      ↓
SIGNAL
      ↓
RELEVANCE ANALYSIS
      ↓
OPPORTUNITY HYPOTHESIS
      ↓
CONFIDENCE
      ↓
POSSIBLE ACTION
```

AI facts aur interpretation ko separate rakhega.

# 14. Signal Decay

A signal gets stale.

Example:

```text
New Service Launch

Detected:
4 months ago
```

Its urgency should decrease unless still relevant.

Concept:

```text
Signal strength
      ↓ over time
```

Different signals have different useful lifetimes.

A meeting request might need action in minutes.

A new market expansion may remain relevant for months.

# 49. Key rule to lock

A **Signal is evidence of change**, not automatically evidence of purchase intent.

Therefore:

> **Signal → Verify → Understand → Determine Relevance → Decide Action**

not:

> **Signal → Spam Email**

Ye difference system ki quality ko massively improve karega.
